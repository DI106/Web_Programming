Explore Infinite Worlds

Every dimension can have different versions of characters, planets and civilizations. Traveling between these worlds makes every adventure unpredictable.

Earth C-137

Earth C-137 is one of the most important dimensions in the Rick and Morty multiverse. It is strongly connected with Rick C-137 and many major events in the series.

Citadel of Ricks

The Citadel of Ricks is a huge space station populated by many different versions of Rick and Morty from different dimensions.

Blips and Chitz

Blips and Chitz is an intergalactic arcade filled with strange games and entertainment from across the universe.

Prime Dimension

The Prime Dimension is an important reality connected to several major characters and events in the series.

Doofus Dimension

The Doofus Dimension is one of the many unusual realities in the multiverse and is associated with an alternative version of Rick.
