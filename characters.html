CHARACTERS

Main Characters

Meet some of the most important and interesting characters from the Rick and Morty universe.

Rick Sanchez

Rick Sanchez is a brilliant scientist and one of the main characters of the series. He travels through different dimensions using portal technology and often takes Morty with him on his adventures.

  • Species: Human
  • Occupation: Scientist
  • Dimension: C-137

Morty Smith

Morty Smith is Rick's grandson. He is a student who often joins Rick during his adventures across different planets and dimensions.

  • Species: Human
  • Occupation: Student
  • Family: Smith Family

Beth Smith

Beth Smith is Rick's daughter and Morty and Summer's mother. She works as a horse surgeon and is an important member of the Smith family.

  • Species: Human
  • Occupation: Horse Surgeon
  • Family: Smith Family

Jerry Smith

Jerry Smith is Rick's son and Morty and Summer's father. He is a student and is often caught in his father's adventures.

  • Species: Human
  • Occupation: Unemployed
  • Family: Smith Family

Summer Smith

Summer Smith is Morty's older sister. She is confident, independent and sometimes joins Rick and Morty on their adventures.

  • Species: Human
  • Occupation: Student
  • Family: Smith Family

Mr. Poopybutthole

Mr. Poopybutthole is a friendly character who has a close relationship with the Smith family. He is known for his cheerful personality and his unusual appearance.

  • Species: Unknown
  • Occupation: Unknown
  • Family: Smith Family Friend
